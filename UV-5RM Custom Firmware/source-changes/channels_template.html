How bands work
  • A band is a set of channels, like Weather or Air. On the radio, press Band to pick one, then hold # to scan it. ALL is always there and scans every channel.
  • A scan covers the band's channels that have Scan ticked. Tick a band's bar to tick all of its channels.
  • New band makes a band, up to 16. Use Add channel on a band's bar to add a channel to it, Edit to rename it, and Delete to remove it.
  • A channel can be in more than one band, so it shows under each one. Click a channel to change its bands. ✕ deletes the channel.
  • Nothing changes on the radio until you click Write to radio.

Quick messages

In TEXT, press VFO/MR to see these. Pick one with Up/Down or its number, then press Menu to send it, or # to change it first. Up to 50 characters each; letters become capitals. The list on the radio shows the first 29 characters and the whole message below it.
